Centerpoint is a small Mac app that finds the visual center of a logo: the point where its visual weight sits, which is often not the middle of its bounding box. Drop in a PNG, JPEG, SVG, PDF, HEIC or TIFF file to see both centers, the offset between them, and export an optically centered PNG.

Frequently asked questions

How is the visual center calculated?
Centerpoint separates the logo from its background (transparency, a solid color, or a gradient) and computes the weighted centroid of the logo's pixels. The Weighting slider controls whether every visible pixel counts equally (Shape) or darker and more opaque areas count more (Tone).

What does the free version include?
Five free analyses. A one-time in-app purchase unlocks unlimited analyses. There is no subscription.

I bought the unlock on another Mac.
Choose Centerpoint › Restore Purchases while signed in with the same Apple Account.

The result looks wrong for my file.
Try a different Background setting in the inspector. Logos with busy photographic backgrounds work best when exported with transparency first.
