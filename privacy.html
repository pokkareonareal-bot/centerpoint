Privacy Policy

SupportPrivacy Policy

Effective October 21, 2026

Centerpoint does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

Your files

Images you open in Centerpoint are analyzed entirely on your Mac. They are never uploaded or sent anywhere. Centerpoint only reads files you choose, and only writes files where you choose to save an export.

Purchases

The optional unlock is handled by Apple through the App Store. Centerpoint receives only confirmation that the purchase is valid; it never sees your payment details. See Apple's Privacy Policy.

Usage count

To enforce the free limit, Centerpoint stores the number of analyses you have run locally on your Mac (in your keychain and app preferences). This number never leaves your device.

Analytics, ads and tracking

Centerpoint contains no analytics, advertising, or tracking of any kind, and makes no network connections other than to Apple's App Store for purchases.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page. Questions: open an issue on GitHub.
